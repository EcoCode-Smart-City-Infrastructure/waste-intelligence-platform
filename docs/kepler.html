What is on the map

  • Bins — fill & status (point): every demo bin, coloured and sized by fill %. Click one for bin, site, borough, status, forecast time-to-full and 24-hour forecast fill.
  • Fill density — 3D grid (grid, off by default): the same bins aggregated into 3D columns whose height and colour follow average fill — press “Fleet 3D” to turn it on and tilt the view.
  • Urgent collection route (arc): the nearest-neighbour route through urgent bins (≥ 80%), exactly as built in Bins & Routes, with leg distance per arc.
  • Live tonnage by borough (point): latest-month DSNY tonnage from Socrata dataset ebb7-mvp5, aggregated by borough and centred on each borough — sized by refuse tonnage. Falls back to the labelled sample only if the live call fails.
  • Bin fill tracking — 48 h (point, off by default): one point per bin per hour of fill history, with a time-range filter — press “Tracking · 48 h” to animate fill evolution over time. History uses the same seeded series and the same forecasting math as the Forecasting view (current-cycle cut on a drop > 30 points).
  • Knowledge graph is not plotted as geography: its nodes have no coordinates, and inventing locations for real facilities would be fabrication. It stays in its own view, linked from the header.

Kepler.gl 3.0.0 is loaded from unpkg (React / ReactDOM / Redux / React-Redux / styled-components are its documented externals, plus react-is, which styled-components itself needs to start). If the CDN is unreachable, this page says so plainly instead of showing an empty map — the main app keeps working without it.
